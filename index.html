Banho - Support

Banho is a film development timer for iOS. Run B&W, C-41 and E-6 processes with step-by-step timers, agitation alerts, temperature compensation and your own custom recipes.

Help

  • Processes: pick a preset (B&W, C-41, E-6) or create your own recipe with custom steps, temperatures and agitation plan.
  • Temperature compensation: enter your measured chemistry temperature before starting and Banho adjusts development time automatically.
  • Custom recipes: swipe left on a recipe in the list to delete it. Tap a recipe to fine-tune step times.
  • Pro: a one-time purchase removes ads and unlocks the dilution calculator, per-development notes and preset fine-tuning. Use "Restore purchases" on the Pro screen if you reinstall.
